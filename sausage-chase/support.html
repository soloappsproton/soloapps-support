Sausage Chase support

A little help for you and your pup.

soloapps@proton.me

Does the game need internet?

No. Every level and both modes work offline. Only the leaderboards and trophies on Game Center need a connection. Scores you earn offline are sent the next time you are online and signed in to Game Center.

What is the difference between the modes?

In a 90-second race, eat more snacks than your rival before the clock runs out. In Endless there is no clock and no rival: every snack makes your pup longer, every third snack makes it faster, and one bump into the arena edge or your own tail ends the run.

How do I steer?

Open Steering in the menu and choose Joystick + touch or Touch only. On Digtagon, Pond and Disco, three snacks charge a four-second Sprint; in Space and the Lagoon they charge a four-second Burst. Tap it when it lights up and keep steering while it runs.

Where are my trophies and the leaderboards?

Open Trophies from the menu to see what you have earned and to open the worldwide and country leaderboards. Your trophies are kept on this device and, if you are signed in, on Game Center too.

Why am I on the wrong country leaderboard?

Endless runs count for the country set in your device’s Region setting (Settings › General › Language & Region). Change it there, and your next runs will count for the new country.

How do I turn the sound off?

During a game, tap the speaker button to mute or unmute game sounds. The game remembers your choice.

How do I report a problem?

Email soloapps@proton.me with what happened, what you expected, your app version, device model and iOS version. Include the level and mode, and a screenshot if you are comfortable sharing it. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.
