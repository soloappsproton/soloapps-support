SAUSAGE CHASE

Privacy policy

A snack race for dachshunds. Your information stays yours.

soloapps@proton.me

We do not collect your information

Sausage Chase does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account of its own. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Your game stays with you

Races and endless runs are played entirely on your device and work without internet. The app has no server of its own and does not send your games to the developer.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your best scores, your trophies and snack totals, the pup, steering and mode you chose, the last rival you raced and whether game sounds are muted. If a leaderboard score cannot be sent right away, the app keeps it with a Game Center player reference, not your name, until it reaches the right Game Center account; it is then removed. This stays on your device until it is replaced or removed with the app. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Leaderboards and trophies

Leaderboards and trophies are optional and use Apple’s Game Center. If you sign in, Apple handles your sign-in and your Game Center name under Apple’s own privacy policy. Your scores and trophies are sent to Game Center, and other players can see your Game Center name and scores on the leaderboards. There is no chat in the app.

Endless runs also count on a leaderboard for the country set in your device’s Region setting (Settings › General › Language & Region). The app reads that setting, not your location. Anyone who views that country’s leaderboard can see your score on it.

When you contact support

If you email soloapps@proton.me, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include are received through Proton Mail and used to respond to your request. Send only the information needed to explain the issue. Support correspondence may be kept while resolving and following up on the request; you can ask for its deletion at the same address.

Your choices

You can play without ever signing in to Game Center. Changing your Region setting changes which country leaderboard your future endless runs count for. To remove locally stored app data and settings, delete the app in Settings; offloading the app keeps its data. Manage any existing device backups separately in your Apple settings. Your Game Center account is managed by Apple in Settings.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact soloapps@proton.me. This policy describes the current version of Sausage Chase. If data handling changes, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated.
