Privacy policy

A quiet game. Clear choices about your information.

soloapps@proton.me

Your game stays with you

Atelier Chess works without an account. Games against Stockfish and two-player games run on your device. The app does not send your moves or saved games to the developer.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your current game, move history, opponent and difficulty settings so you can resume playing. It also remembers preferences such as board rotation, sounds and haptics. These files remain until replaced by a new game or removed with the app. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

No advertising or tracking

The current app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect identifiers, location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings. No personal information is automatically sent to the developer by the app.

When you contact support

If you email soloapps@proton.me, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include are received through Proton Mail and used to respond to your request. Send only the information needed to explain the issue. Support correspondence may be kept while resolving and following up on the request; you can ask for its deletion at the same address.

Links and sharing

External links open services with their own privacy policies. Source-code exports and support messages are shared only when you choose to do so. Your email provider, Apple and any service you open may process information under their own policies.

Your choices

You can play offline and choose whether to contact support or open external links. Starting a new game replaces the current saved game. To remove locally stored app data and preferences, delete the app in Settings; offloading the app keeps its data. Manage any existing device backups separately in your Apple settings.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact soloapps@proton.me. This policy describes the current version of Atelier Chess. If data handling changes, the policy and App Store disclosures will be updated.
