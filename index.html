Atelier Chess support

A little guidance, whenever you need it.

soloapps@proton.me

Does computer play need internet?

No. Stockfish runs on your device. Choose New game from Game options, select Computer, and pick a difficulty before starting.

Will my game be saved?

Your current game and move history are saved automatically on this device. Starting a new game replaces that saved game. There is no account or in-app cloud sync.

How do I rotate the board?

Use the rotate button to turn the playing surface. Game options also includes Flip board and, after a manual rotation, an option to restore the default orientation.

How do I change sounds and haptics?

Open Game options using the three-dot button, then switch Move sounds or Haptics on or off.

How do I report a problem?

Email soloapps@proton.me with what happened, what you expected, your app version, device model and iOS version. If the issue concerns a move, include the move history or a screenshot if you are comfortable sharing it. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

Where are the engine credits and source?

Open Game options → Engine & credits for the Stockfish and CHA-Solver acknowledgments, licenses and source export.
