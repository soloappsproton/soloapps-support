Privacy policy

A quiet game. Your information stays yours.

soloapps@proton.me

We do not collect your information

Ocean Chess does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account of its own. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Your game stays with you

Games against Rookline, the built-in opponent, and pass and play run entirely on your device and work without internet. The app does not send your moves or saved games to the developer.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your current game, move history, opponent and difficulty so you can resume playing. It also remembers your sound and Day or Night settings. For an online game it also stores the game’s Game Center reference and your opponent’s Game Center name. This stays on your device until it is replaced by a new game or removed with the app. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Online games

Online play is optional and uses Apple’s Game Center. If you choose it, Apple handles your sign-in, your Game Center name, finding an opponent and turn notifications, under Apple’s own privacy policy. Your moves and each game’s result pass through Game Center so that you and your opponent can play, and your opponent sees your Game Center name. The developer does not receive your Game Center details, and there is no chat in the app.

When you contact support

If you email soloapps@proton.me, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include are received through Proton Mail and used to respond to your request. Send only the information needed to explain the issue. Support correspondence may be kept while resolving and following up on the request; you can ask for its deletion at the same address.

Your choices

You can play offline and never use Game Center. To remove locally stored app data and settings, delete the app in Settings; offloading the app keeps its data. Manage any existing device backups separately in your Apple settings. Your Game Center account is managed by Apple in Settings.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact soloapps@proton.me. This policy describes the current version of Ocean Chess. If data handling changes, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated.
