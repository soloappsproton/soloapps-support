Does the game need internet?

Only for Online games. Playing against Rookline and pass and play both work offline.

How do online games work?

Open New game, choose Online, then Find a game. Sign in to Game Center if asked, then invite someone or let Game Center find an opponent. Take your turn whenever you like. The game tells you when it is your move.

Will my game be saved?

Your current game and move history are saved automatically on this device. Starting a new game replaces that saved game. Online games stay in Game Center, so you can return to them from New game.

How do I move around the board?

Drag to orbit the board and pinch to zoom. The viewfinder button resets the view. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square, to move.

How do I change the sound or lighting?

Use the speaker button for sound and the sun or moon button to switch between Day and Night.

How do I report a problem?

Email soloapps@proton.me with what happened, what you expected, your app version, device model and iOS version. If the issue concerns a move, include the move history or a screenshot if you are comfortable sharing it. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.
